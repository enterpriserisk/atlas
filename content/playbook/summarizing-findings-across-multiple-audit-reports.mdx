## When to use this

You have several completed audit reports — across departments, or over multiple cycles — and need a combined view of recurring findings or common themes for a portfolio-level report.

## How to do it well

- Ground the tool in the actual report documents (U-M Maizey configured with your reports as sources) rather than asking it to recall findings from memory.
- Ask it to group similar findings across reports and note how many reports each recurring finding appeared in.
- Ask for the combined view to cite which specific report each finding came from, so you can trace it back.
- Verify a sample of the groupings against the original reports before using the rollup in a portfolio summary.

## Watch out for

- Findings that sound similar across reports may actually be about different underlying issues — check before merging them.
- A rollup can flatten important context, like severity or department-specific factors, that mattered in the original report.
- Audit reports often contain sensitive findings — use only an approved, appropriately grounded tool.

## Example

You configure U-M Maizey with five departmental audit reports and ask for findings that recur in three or more. It surfaces a recurring access-control finding; you confirm it's genuinely the same underlying issue in each report before including it as a portfolio-level theme.
