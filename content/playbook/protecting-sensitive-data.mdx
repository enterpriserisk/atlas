## When to use this

Any task where the material might include personal, health, financial, or otherwise regulated or confidential information. What you put into a prompt matters as much as what you get out.

## How to do it well

- Identify the data's classification before choosing a tool.
- Use only tools approved for that classification (confirm current guidance with ITS).
- Remove or anonymize identifiers when they aren't needed for the task.
- When unsure, summarize in the aggregate and leave specifics out.

## Watch out for

- Pasting spreadsheets or records with identifying columns into a general tool.
- Assuming a "private" or "enterprise" mode is automatically approved for all data.
- Credentials, keys, or passwords appearing in prompts.

## Example

Instead of pasting a roster with names and IDs to analyze trends, you remove the identifying columns and ask only about the aggregate patterns — getting the insight without exposing personal data.
