## When to use this

You've completed a detailed risk assessment or audit finding and need a short, non-technical version for a dean, VP, or other leadership audience who needs the takeaway, not the methodology.

## How to do it well

- Give the tool your finished, reviewed assessment as source material — don't ask it to assess the risk itself.
- Ask explicitly for the audience and format: e.g., "three bullet points a non-technical VP could read in 30 seconds."
- Ask it to preserve the severity and urgency language from the original rather than softening or amplifying it.
- Have someone who understands the underlying finding review the simplified version before it goes out.

## Watch out for

- Simplification can accidentally drop a caveat or condition that changes the meaning (e.g., "high risk if X occurs" becoming just "high risk").
- Watch for the tool softening urgent language to sound more polished — leadership needs to feel the actual stakes.
- Never include information in the prompt beyond what's appropriate for the eventual audience of the draft.
