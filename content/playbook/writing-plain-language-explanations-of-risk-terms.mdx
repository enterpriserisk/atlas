## When to use this

You're writing an FAQ, training aid, or general communication and need to explain a technical risk, compliance, or security term to an audience without specialized background.

## How to do it well

- Ask for a short, jargon-free explanation plus one concrete, relatable example.
- Specify the audience — e.g., "for a new staff member with no compliance background."
- Ask the tool to avoid introducing new jargon while explaining the original term.
- Keep a consistent glossary of your own approved plain-language explanations for terms you use often.

## Watch out for

- Oversimplification can lose an important nuance or exception that actually matters for compliance.
- Verify the explanation is still technically accurate, not just easy to read — a friendly explanation that's wrong is worse than a dense one that's right.
- Don't present the AI-drafted explanation as official institutional guidance without review if it touches a compliance-sensitive term.

## Example

You ask U-M GPT to explain "data classification" in plain language for a new-hire FAQ. You review the draft, confirm it doesn't oversimplify away the distinction between confidential and regulated data, and add it to your onboarding materials.
