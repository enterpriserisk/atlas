## When to use this

You're adding a new risk to your risk register or updating an existing entry, and want help structuring the description, mitigation, and ownership fields clearly and consistently.

## How to do it well

- Provide your own understanding of the risk, its context, and any existing mitigations; ask the tool to help you phrase and structure the entry clearly.
- Keep the format consistent with your register's standard fields (description, category, owner, mitigation, status).
- Ask the tool to flag if the description and the stated mitigation don't seem to logically connect.
- Have the risk owner assign or confirm the actual likelihood and impact ratings — never let AI generate those ratings.

## Watch out for

- Never delegate the actual risk rating (likelihood × impact) to AI — that's a judgment call requiring institutional context it doesn't have.
- A well-written but inaccurate description can make a risk look better understood than it actually is — verify the substance, not just the prose.
- Keep the register's ownership and review-date fields current per your governance process.

## Example

You give U-M GPT rough notes about a new vendor concentration risk and ask for a structured draft entry matching your register's format. The risk owner reviews it, assigns the likelihood and impact ratings themselves, and adjusts the mitigation description for accuracy before it's added to the register.
