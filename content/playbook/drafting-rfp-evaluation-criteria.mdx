## When to use this

You're preparing to evaluate vendor proposals or RFP responses and need a structured scoring rubric or list of evaluation criteria drafted from your requirements.

## How to do it well

- Give the tool your actual requirements and constraints; ask it to organize them into weighted categories (security, cost, support, implementation timeline, etc.).
- Ask for criteria phrased as objective, scorable questions rather than vague qualities.
- Ask it to flag any requirement that's ambiguous or hard to score objectively as written.
- Have your procurement lead and relevant subject-matter reviewers finalize weights and thresholds — that's a judgment call, not a drafting task.

## Watch out for

- AI may suggest criteria or weights that sound reasonable but don't reflect your institution's actual priorities.
- Don't let a generated rubric silently drop a compliance-mandated requirement — check it against your procurement policy.
- Keep proprietary vendor information related to a live procurement out of any tool not approved for it, especially during an active competitive process.

## Example

You give U-M GPT your five must-have requirements for a new records-management vendor and ask for a weighted scoring rubric with objective scoring questions for each category. Your procurement lead adjusts two weights and adds a compliance requirement the draft didn't include before it's used to score responses.
