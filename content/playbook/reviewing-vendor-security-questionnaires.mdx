## When to use this

You're reviewing a vendor's completed security or privacy questionnaire (a SIG, CAIQ, or a custom U-M form) and need to quickly identify weak or missing answers before a deeper manual review.

## How to do it well

- Paste the vendor's actual answers (or ground the tool in the document via U-M Maizey) — don't ask the tool to guess at "typical" vendor practices.
- Ask it to flag specific things: blank answers, "no" answers to standard controls (encryption at rest, MFA, etc.), and any answer that contradicts another part of the form.
- Ask for the flags organized by risk area (data protection, access control, incident response) so a reviewer can triage quickly.
- Treat the output as a checklist of things to verify — not a scored risk rating.

## Watch out for

- A vendor's own answers may be incomplete or optimistic — AI can't verify a vendor is telling the truth, only flag what's on the page.
- Don't let AI assign a final risk tier or approval recommendation; that judgment call belongs to a risk analyst.
- Vendor questionnaires often contain proprietary or confidential information — use a University-approved tool.

## Example

You paste a vendor's completed security questionnaire into U-M Maizey and ask it to list every question answered "no" or left blank, grouped by risk area. You use that list to focus your manual follow-up questions instead of re-reading all 120 questions line by line.
